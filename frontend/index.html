How it works from your reading to a new paper

  1. Read

    Reads every page of each paper and picks out its key ideas, each with a quote checked word for word.

    Muse Spark 1.3

  2. Translate

    Rewrites each idea in plain words that any field can understand.

    Muse Spark 1.3

  3. Relate

    Finds how each pair of papers relates, explains it, and backs it with quotes checked word for word.

    Muse Spark 1.3

  4. Challenge

    A different AI model tries to prove each link wrong. The strongest that survive get a final check.

    Muse Spark Open · Muse Wonder

  5. Plan

    Turns the links that held up into a paper you could write, then searches published work before calling it new.

    Muse Spark 1.3 · web search

      The models, and their one job each
      • Meta Model APIMuse Spark 1.3Reads and explains the papers, finds the links and plans new papers. With web search, it checks published work before an idea is called new.
      • Muse Spark OpenThe skeptic: a different model that tries to break every link before you see it.
      • Meta Model APIMuse WonderThe final judge: gives the strongest links a last check. It is also the skeptic for links that involve your own PDFs.
      • Meta Model APISAM 3.1Points at the proof: in a figure behind a quote, it outlines the real things the quote is about, like the cars in a traffic experiment.

      How Synapse keeps itself honest

      1. Research only

        Synapse keeps to scholarly material, in what you add and in everything it suggests back to you.

      2. Every quote is real

        Each quote is searched for word for word in the paper. If it isn't there, the link is thrown out.

      3. A second AI argues back

        A different model tries to break every link before you see it.

      4. Safety check before reading

        Every link and PDF is checked before any AI sees it: public pages only, hidden text removed, and a checker model flags text that tries to give the AI orders.

      5. Sources are labelled

        Peer-reviewed, preprint or blog, so you know how much weight each one deserves.

      6. New only after a search

        An idea is called new only after a real search of published work, and the closest papers it found are shown with it.

      Your papers stay yours

      Sent only where needed. Paper text goes only to Meta's models, over encrypted connections. Public papers also go to Muse Spark Open on Baseten for the challenge. Nothing about your computer is sent.
      Yours to remove. A PDF you add is kept under a name made from its contents, never your file name, and deleted when you remove it. Models that train on what they're sent are never used.
      Short quotes, always cited. Synapse shows only brief excerpts, with the title, page and a link to the source.
