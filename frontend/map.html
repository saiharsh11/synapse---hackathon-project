What Synapse accepts

Synapse works with research from any field. Links can come from established journals, major conference proceedings and arXiv; for anything else, upload the PDF. Anything that isn't research is set aside. Its suggestions follow the same standard: every paper it recommends comes from an established journal, a major conference or arXiv.
